# UX Research

Produce ethical, decision-relevant evidence about real users without treating opinions,
personas, analytics, or a polished prototype as substitutes for research.

## Essentials

- **Start from research questions** — turn assumptions and pending decisions into
  prioritized questions before choosing a method
- **Recruit for relevant variation** — define behaviors, contexts, access needs,
  exclusion risks, and sample limitations rather than seeking an average user
- **Protect participants and data** — obtain informed consent, minimize collection,
  separate identities, restrict access, define retention, and support withdrawal
- **Observe before interpreting** — capture behavior, quotes, context, task outcome,
  and researcher influence separately from findings
- **Test tasks, not participants** — use realistic goals, neutral prompts, accessible
  sessions, and consistent prototypes without coaching toward success
- **Return evidence with limits** — connect each finding to observations, affected
  users, confidence, contradictions, design implication, and unanswered questions

## Gotchas

- Five participants are not a universal saturation rule; sample size follows research
  questions, participant diversity, risk, and observed repetition.
- Asking whether users like a proposed feature predicts politeness better than behavior.
- Analytics shows what happened in an instrumented population, not why it happened or
  who was excluded.
- A researcher-created persona without evidence is an assumption with a name and photo.
- Research findings inform design and product decisions; they do not approve a design
  or prove that every user can use it.

## Example

```text
Question: Can first-time users recover from an identity-photo rejection?
Participants: Six likely users across device age, confidence, language, and access need.
Task: Complete verification after a realistic rejection without facilitator coaching.
Observation: Four repeat the same capture because the rejection reason is not actionable.
Finding: Recovery guidance does not support a successful next attempt.
Limitation: No switch-control participant; remote setup excluded low-connectivity use.
Action: Test reason-specific guidance and preserve the two coverage gaps.
```
