# Sources

## Plan user research

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/plan-user-research-for-your-service
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `SKILL.md`, `references/plan-and-recruit.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Start from prioritized research questions, choose methods that
  can answer them efficiently, involve the multidisciplinary team, and update the plan
  as evidence changes.

## Recruit participants

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/find-user-research-participants
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `references/plan-and-recruit.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Define recruitment criteria from relevant user
  characteristics and include disabled participants and necessary participation support.

## Participant privacy

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/managing-user-research-data-participant-privacy
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `SKILL.md`, `references/plan-and-recruit.md`, `references/conduct-and-analyse.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Treat notes, recordings, prototype inputs, and participant
  details as protected research data with explicit collection, access, retention, and
  handling boundaries.

## Analyse research

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/analyse-a-research-session
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `references/conduct-and-analyse.md`, `references/usability-and-handoff.md`

## Research through delivery

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/user-research-in-alpha
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
