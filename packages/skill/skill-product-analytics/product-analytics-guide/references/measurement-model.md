# Measurement Model

## Decision Frame

Record:

```text
Decision and owner:
User or business outcome:
Population and important segments:
Baseline and evidence period:
Alternatives and expected mechanism:
Decision horizon:
Action thresholds and guardrails:
Qualitative evidence required:
```

Use goals, signals, and metrics to prevent immediately selecting an available event as
the KPI. A HEART-style prompt can check happiness, engagement, adoption, retention, and
task success, but select only dimensions that reflect the decision and product.

## Metric Contract

For every metric define:

```text
Name, purpose, and owner:
Numerator and denominator:
Eligible population and exclusions:
Unit of analysis and identity:
Source events or records and versions:
Attribution and deduplication:
Window, lateness, and aggregation:
Segments:
Expected direction and practical threshold:
Guardrails:
Known biases and limitations:
```

Balance leading behavior with downstream outcomes and harms. Combine analytics with
research, support, operational, financial, and accessibility evidence when the service
question crosses those channels. Never infer individual intent from aggregate behavior.
