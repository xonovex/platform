# Sources

## User-centered metrics

- **URL:** https://research.google/pubs/measuring-the-user-experience-on-a-large-scale-user-centered-metrics-for-web-applications/
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `SKILL.md`, `references/measurement-model.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Use the HEART framework as a user-centered completeness prompt
  and map product goals to signals and measurable metrics for decision-making.

## Measuring service success

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/measuring-the-success-of-your-service
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `SKILL.md`, `references/measurement-model.md`,
  `references/experiments-and-decisions.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Match measurement to the service and end-to-end journey,
  combine performance metrics with research, and use multiple operational, support,
  financial, and user evidence sources.

## Controlled experimentation

- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/online-experimentation-at-microsoft/
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `references/experiments-and-decisions.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Use randomized controlled experiments to estimate causal
  product effects, support evidence-driven product decisions, and treat trustworthy
  experimental execution as both a technical and cultural capability.

## Privacy risk management

- **URL:** https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `references/instrumentation-and-quality.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Identify and manage privacy risk while designing and operating
  data-processing products and services rather than treating privacy as a post-hoc
  compliance check.
