# Sources

## Discovery phase

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-discovery-phase-works
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `SKILL.md`, `references/opportunity-framing.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Reframe prescribed solutions as problems, define discovery
  goals and scope, understand constraints, and decide whether further investment is
  justified without building during discovery.

## User needs

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `references/opportunity-framing.md`, `references/prioritization-and-handoff.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Base needs on research rather than assumptions, keep them
  solution-neutral, include the wider journey, and preserve traceability into later
  product definition.

## Measuring success

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/measuring-the-success-of-your-service
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `SKILL.md`, `references/opportunity-framing.md`, `references/prioritization-and-handoff.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Match measures to the outcome and journey, combine performance
  data with research, and use several evidence sources rather than one analytics signal.

## Testing risky assumptions

- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-alpha-phase-works
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24
- **References:** `references/experiments-and-evidence.md`
- **Aspects extracted:** Test the riskiest assumptions with the minimum prototype or
  feasibility work needed to support a decision, and expect discovery artifacts to be
  disposable.
