# Prioritization and Handoff

Compare opportunities or interventions against the same explicit dimensions:

- outcome contribution and affected population;
- strength and freshness of evidence;
- whole-journey fit and accessibility;
- feasibility, dependencies, and operational burden;
- cost of delay and implementation cost;
- downside risk, reversibility, and learning value.

Do not hide uncertainty inside one synthetic score. Show the evidence behind each
dimension, sensitivity to disputed assumptions, and material dissent.

Return:

```markdown
## Recommendation

Outcome: proceed | change | pause | stop

## Evidence relied on

## Options and tradeoffs

## Assumptions tested and remaining

## Product-definition handoff

User needs:
Success measures:
Guardrails:
Constraints:
Open research:
Related evidence:
```

The handoff defines what product definition must preserve. It does not create user
stories, an implementation plan, a funding approval, or a roadmap commitment.
