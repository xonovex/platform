# Opportunity Framing

1. State the initiating request and remove its implied solution.
2. Identify the people trying to make progress, including users who receive, support,
   administer, or are excluded by the current service.
3. Describe the current journey, observed behavior, pain, workaround, frequency, and
   consequence using evidence rather than personas invented from assumptions.
4. Set the opportunity boundary: included journey, excluded journey, constraints,
   dependencies, and why now.
5. Define the desired outcome with baseline, target, population, measurement window,
   leading indicator, lagging indicator, and guardrails.
6. List existing interventions and non-software alternatives before proposing new
   delivery.

Use this compact output:

```markdown
## Opportunity

## Users and current journey

## Evidence and unknowns

## Outcome and guardrails

## Scope, constraints, and alternatives
```

Keep user needs solution-neutral. “Receive a timely reminder” is a need; “receive a
push notification” is one possible intervention.
