# Debrief and Evidence

Debrief soon after the session:

1. Restate the charter and exact subject revision.
2. Review the session timeline and distinguish observations from conclusions.
3. Reproduce material findings when safe; record intermittent behavior honestly.
4. Classify findings as defects, risks, questions, environment problems, testability
   problems, or improvements.
5. Map explored and untested dimensions.
6. Record blocked areas and why evidence is unavailable.
7. Propose the smallest follow-up charter, automated check, scripted test, or product
   clarification that reduces the remaining risk.

Return:

```markdown
## Charter outcome

## Findings

- Observation:
  Oracle:
  Reproduction:
  Evidence:
  Consequence:
  Confidence:

## Coverage

Explored:
Not explored:
Blocked:

## New risks and questions

## Follow-up
```

Do not emit a universal pass or fail unless the caller supplied binding criteria. For
release validation, attach findings and coverage to those criteria while preserving
the exploratory session's limitations.
