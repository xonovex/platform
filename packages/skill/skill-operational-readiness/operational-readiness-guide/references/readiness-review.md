# Readiness Review

## Tailored Review

Build review questions from critical service functions, architecture, threats,
obligations, prior incidents, near misses, recurring support work, and relevant
organizational standards. Keep the high-consequence set small enough to examine with
evidence; a copied universal checklist is not a review.

Include architecture and dependencies, objectives, capacity, observability, release
quality, event management, recovery, security operations, data lifecycle, maintenance,
support, people, access, training, cost, and sustainability where relevant.

## Finding Contract

```text
Service subject and revision:
Readiness criterion:
Evidence and last exercise:
Status: met | gap | risk accepted | not applicable | unknown
Failure consequence:
Mitigation or action:
Owner and target:
Residual risk and accountable owner:
Re-review trigger:
```

Return ready, conditionally ready, not ready, or blocked. A mitigation is not completed
evidence. Keep accepted risk explicit and time-bounded.

Run the review before launch or ownership transfer, during major design changes, after
significant incidents or near misses, and periodically according to service risk.
Feed learned failure modes back into the review questions. Release readiness consumes
the result for a candidate; incident response owns active events.
