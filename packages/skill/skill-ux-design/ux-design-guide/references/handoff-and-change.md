# Handoff and Change

## Design Decision Record

Capture:

```text
Design subject and revision:
Users, need, journey, and outcome:
Research and constraints:
Alternatives considered:
Chosen approach and rationale:
States and content:
Accessibility requirements:
Evaluation evidence:
Known limitations and unresolved questions:
Related product, architecture, and test references:
```

Reference provider-native design files by stable URL, node or frame identity, and
version when available. A screenshot alone is not an inspectable interaction contract.

## Implementation Handoff

Walk engineering, QA, content, accessibility, product, and operations through the
states and rationale. Convert expected behavior into traceable stories and acceptance
examples without duplicating them inside the design artifact. Identify analytics,
privacy, performance, and operational implications for their owning capabilities.

During implementation, review material deviations against the user need and tested
behavior. Update the design decision and its revision rather than silently making the
prototype match the build. Keep final accessibility, quality, and release validation
independent from design sign-off.
