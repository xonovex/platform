# Drivers and Scenarios

## Evaluation Charter

Record the architecture subject and exact revision, system boundary, relevant
environments, dependencies, business drivers, constraints, stakeholders, decision
deadline, evidence sources, and exclusions. Include product, engineering, security,
operations, data, support, compliance, and affected-user perspectives as applicable.

Use a product quality model as a completeness prompt, not a universal priority list.
Prioritize qualities from the actual business and mission drivers.

## Quality-Attribute Scenario

Write each scenario as:

```text
Source:
Stimulus:
Environment:
Affected artifact:
Expected response:
Response measure:
Priority and rationale:
Evidence available:
```

Prefer thresholds, distributions, recovery objectives, change effort, failure
containment, or observable behavior over adjectives. Include normal and stress
conditions. Capture tensions between scenarios instead of optimizing one quality in
isolation.

## Prioritization

Rank scenarios separately by business importance and implementation or evidence risk.
The combination identifies where analysis is most valuable. Preserve minority and
specialist concerns; consensus is not a prerequisite for documenting a driver.
