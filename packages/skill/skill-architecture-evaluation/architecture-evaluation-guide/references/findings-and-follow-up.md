# Findings and Follow-Up

## Finding Contract

Each finding includes:

```text
Architecture subject and revision:
Affected driver and scenarios:
Type: risk | non-risk | sensitivity | tradeoff | evidence gap
Claim and causal path:
Evidence and freshness:
Severity or decision consequence:
Confidence and limitations:
Mitigation, alternative, or experiment:
Owner and target:
Re-evaluation trigger:
```

Distinguish evidence gaps from demonstrated failures. Rank risk using consequence,
likelihood or exposure, and reversibility appropriate to the organization; do not
invent precision.

## Recommendation

Return proceed, proceed with bounded risks, revise, gather evidence, or reject for the
evaluated decision. Show unresolved scenarios, stakeholder dissent, and the conditions
under which the recommendation changes. The accountable owner records acceptance or
selection through the normal decision and provider process.

Re-evaluate after a material driver, dependency, architecture, load profile, threat
model, operating model, or evidence change. Reference the prior evaluation and exact
new subject revision.
