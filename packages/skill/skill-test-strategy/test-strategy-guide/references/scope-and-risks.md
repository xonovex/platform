# Scope and Risks

## Test Basis

Pin the subject and revision. Gather product outcomes, user stories and examples,
design states, architecture scenarios and dependencies, threat model, regulatory or
accessibility criteria, historical defects, operational incidents, supported
platforms, rollout plan, and known technical constraints. Mark missing or stale inputs.

## Product-Risk Model

For each meaningful failure:

```text
Failure mode:
Affected users, assets, or operations:
Trigger and environment:
Consequence:
Likelihood or exposure:
Detectability and existing controls:
Priority and rationale:
Owning requirement, scenario, or evidence:
```

Use ordinal judgments with rationale unless real data supports more precision. Include
rare catastrophic failures, common friction, data integrity, privacy, accessibility,
security, interoperability, performance, resilience, recovery, and operability where
relevant.

Map each material risk to planned evidence or an explicit residual-risk decision.
Traceability is for revealing gaps and impact, not producing a large matrix for its own
sake.
