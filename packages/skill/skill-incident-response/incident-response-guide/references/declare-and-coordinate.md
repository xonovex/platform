# Declare and Coordinate

## Declaration

Record:

- incident reference, declared time, and estimated start;
- observed user or business impact and affected scope;
- current symptoms, known-good and known-bad revisions, and evidence references;
- severity rationale and escalation conditions;
- incident commander, operations lead, communications lead, specialists, and scribe;
- response channel, effect log, stakeholder channel, and next update time.

Declare from credible impact or risk; do not wait for a confirmed cause. Mark uncertain
facts explicitly.

## Command

The incident commander:

1. Maintains the high-level state, priorities, roles, and decision log.
2. Keeps operations focused on mitigation and prevents uncoordinated system changes.
3. Requests specialists for bounded questions and integrates their evidence.
4. Separates internal investigation from external status communication.
5. Escalates authority, privacy, legal, safety, or security needs to accountable owners.
6. Arranges a named handoff before leaving command.

A handoff states current impact, active mitigation, system divergence, recent effects,
unknown effects, open hypotheses, next checks, communication deadline, and exact owner
of every role.
