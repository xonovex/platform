# solution-design-accept: Accept a Solution Design Revision

1. Resolve the exact Solution Design revision, critique/evidence references, unresolved risks, and profile requirements.
2. Verify the accountable actor plus any architecture, security, privacy, safety, operational, or regulated qualification and authority.
3. Present consequences, limitations, conditions, expiry/review triggers, and missing evidence. A model can prepare but not make an authority-bound decision.
4. Obtain the authorized accept, reject, or conditional decision.
5. Publish the decision against the exact design revision with authority and evidence references.

Apply [early-lifecycle-contracts.md](early-lifecycle-contracts.md). Acceptance is separate from revision.
