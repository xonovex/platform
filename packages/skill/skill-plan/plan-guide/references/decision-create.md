# decision-create: Create an Authority-Bound Decision Result

## Core workflow

1. Resolve the subject, evidence, Design, Formulation, Research, and prior Decision references using [early-lifecycle-contracts.md](early-lifecycle-contracts.md).
2. Name one decision and its scope. If several decisions are coupled, order them by dependency and present only the first.
3. Separate evidence, inference, policy applicability, and authority. Record contradictory or missing evidence and the freshness of material sources.
4. Present viable options, consequences, reversibility, risks, and one honest recommendation. A bounded model or agent may prepare the brief within declared bounds.
5. Verify the decision actor and ordinary or qualified authority required by the profile. Stop for that actor when authority is human; never fabricate consent or qualification.
6. Publish a Decision result containing decision, options, rationale, consequences, authority reference, status, source references, and supersession relationship.

## Status

Use proposed or pending while authority is absent. Use decided/accepted only after the authorized actor responds. Evidence quality and decision authority remain separate evaluations.
