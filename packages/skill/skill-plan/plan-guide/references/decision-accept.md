# decision-accept: Record an Authorized Decision

1. Resolve the exact proposed Decision revision, material evidence, critiques, policy applicability, and any prior decision it supersedes.
2. Verify the named actor, required qualification, delegated authority, scope, and freshness. A model or agent is never a substitute for required human authority.
3. Present the options, recommendation, evidence limits, consequences, conditions, and expiry/review triggers without hiding dissent.
4. Obtain the authorized decision and record its rationale or explicit adoption of the prepared rationale.
5. Publish accepted, rejected, deferred, or conditional status against the exact revision, preserving authority/evidence references and supersession.

Apply [early-lifecycle-contracts.md](early-lifecycle-contracts.md). The authority action does not rewrite its evidence.
