# decision-critique: Critique a Decision Result

1. Resolve the exact Decision revision, evidence, authority, policy, and supersession references.
2. Start fresh independent context; do not act as the decision authority.
3. Challenge scope, options, evidence quality/freshness, recommendation, consequences, reversibility, conflicts of interest, and claimed authority.
4. Report concrete findings with severity, confidence, and the evidence or authority gap that supports each.
5. Publish the critique separately against the exact revision. Do not revise, accept, reject, or supersede the Decision.
