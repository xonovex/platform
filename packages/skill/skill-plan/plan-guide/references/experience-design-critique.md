# experience-design-critique: Critique an Experience Design

1. Resolve the exact Experience Design native revision and its source/validation references.
2. Start fresh independent context; do not reuse the authoring agent's unsupported conclusions.
3. Apply the selected critique methods and applicable privacy, accessibility, safety, or regulated review criteria.
4. Report concrete findings with severity, evidence, affected users/tasks, uncertainty, and suggested direction.
5. Publish the critique separately and relate it to the exact subject revision. Do not revise or accept the design.

Follow the independence and publication rules in [early-lifecycle-contracts.md](early-lifecycle-contracts.md).
