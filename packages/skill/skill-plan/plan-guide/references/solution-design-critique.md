# solution-design-critique: Critique a Solution Design

1. Resolve the exact Solution Design native revision and its source, evidence, and decision references.
2. Start fresh independent context and select relevant architecture, security, privacy, operability, data, or regulated review capabilities.
3. Test boundaries, interfaces, flows, qualities, failure behavior, assumptions, alternatives, and unresolved risks against concrete cases.
4. Report severity-ranked findings with evidence, confidence, affected qualities, and suggested direction.
5. Publish the critique separately against the exact subject revision. Do not revise the design or claim architecture authority.
