# decision-revise: Revise a Decision Brief or Result

1. Resolve the exact Decision revision and every feedback, critique, evidence, policy, and authority reference.
2. Enumerate revision items and preserve the original actor's decision separately from changes to the brief.
3. Update scope, options, evidence, rationale, consequences, conditions, status, and unresolved gaps. Reopen the decision when material evidence, policy, or subject revision changed.
4. Do not transfer or fabricate authority; obtain a new authority action when the revision changes the decision meaning.
5. Publish a new Decision native revision with source and supersession relationships.
