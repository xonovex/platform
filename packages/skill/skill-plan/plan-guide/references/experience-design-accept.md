# experience-design-accept: Accept an Experience Design Revision

1. Resolve the exact Experience Design revision, independent critique, validation evidence, unresolved findings, and profile requirements.
2. Verify the accountable actor and required accessibility, privacy, product, safety, or regulated authority references.
3. Present limitations, conditions, expiry/review triggers, and stale or missing evidence. A model may prepare this brief but cannot claim the decision.
4. Obtain the authorized accept, reject, or conditional decision.
5. Publish that decision provider-natively against the exact design revision and return its authority/evidence references.

Apply [early-lifecycle-contracts.md](early-lifecycle-contracts.md). Acceptance does not mutate the design revision.
