# experience-design-revise: Revise an Experience Design

1. Resolve the exact accepted input revision plus every feedback, critique, validation, and decision reference.
2. Enumerate each change request and resolve, defer with rationale/owner, or reject with authority; do not silently drop feedback.
3. Update interaction, content, accessibility, representation, validation, assumptions, and open questions consistently.
4. Re-run affected validation or mark its evidence stale.
5. Publish a new Experience Design native revision, link supersession and source references, and leave status proposed until accepted.

Apply [early-lifecycle-contracts.md](early-lifecycle-contracts.md); never overwrite the prior revision invisibly.
