- **Used for:** `SKILL.md`, `references/risk-and-impact.md`, `references/data-and-inventory.md`, `references/evaluation-and-monitoring.md`

## Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

- **URL:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-16
