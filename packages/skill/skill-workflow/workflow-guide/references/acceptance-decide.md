# acceptance-decide: Record Human Acceptance

## Core workflow

1. Resolve the exact Deliverable Publication revision, intended target, fresh acceptance
   evidence package, current policy/profile version, open conditions, and the profile's
   actor requirements. Use **agent-governance-guide** for the actor record, what a role
   string may mean, independence, segregation of duties, and which of them code enforces.
2. Present source evidence and limitations without turning agent or model summaries into
   authoritative facts. Require the accountable human to identify themselves through the
   selected provider's authoritative mechanism.
3. Record `accept`, `accept-with-conditions`, or `reject`, rationale, conditions, scope,
   decision time, expiry, and the exact subject, target, evidence, and policy bindings.
4. Publish an Acceptance result through the selected provider and re-resolve its native
   reference/revision. Preserve the human decision evidence separately from advisory
   preparation and automated checks.
5. Report that changed bindings or expiry invalidate future use. Acceptance grants no
   target mutation by itself; Integration must validate it again at an external control
   point.

Reject attempts by a script, model, agent, unqualified actor, self-reviewer where
independence is required, or copied provider identity to create the accountable decision.
