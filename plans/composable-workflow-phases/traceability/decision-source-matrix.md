# Decision–Source Matrix

Last verified: **2026-07-14**

Decision classifications:

- **law/standard/framework-derived** — the decision operationalizes a stated obligation or control family, subject to applicability and source limitations.
- **platform-derived** — the decision reflects documented native platform behavior and still requires conformance tests.
- **source-aligned architectural synthesis** — sources support the concern or constraint, while Xonovex defines the concrete architecture.
- **architectural synthesis / scope decision** — an original Xonovex design choice; sources may inform it but do not mandate it.
