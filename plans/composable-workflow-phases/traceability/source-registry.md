# Authoritative Source Registry

Last verified: **2026-07-14**

This registry classifies sources by authority and records the scope and limits of claims they can support. A source being listed does not make every Xonovex decision a requirement of that source.

## Source hierarchy

1. Binding law and regulation
2. International standards and normative public recommendations
3. Government frameworks and official guidance
4. Vendor primary documentation
5. Industry specifications and authoritative guidance
6. Architectural/research guidance

## Verification rules

- Laws require an applicability analysis and review of the current consolidated text. Article references are not legal advice.
- ISO public landing pages support broad scope claims only. Normative clause-level claims require the licensed standard.
- Vendor capabilities require a version-pinned conformance test; documentation alone is not proof that an adapter works.
- Industry crosswalks and framework mappings are contextual and do not imply equivalence, certification or compliance.
- Draft or evolving specifications must be labeled and pinned when selected.
